# PHASE 1 — MASTER SCRIPT PROMPT (SHORT VIDEO, 80–100 s)

Channel format: **"La tua vita a ogni livello come [PROFESSIONE]"**
Market: Italy. Spoken language: Italian. Delivered in 9:16 and 16:9.

---

## INPUT

PROFESSION (Italian name + English name):
[es. Elettricista (Electrician)]

TARGET LANGUAGE:
Italian (native, spoken, natural; never a translation feel).

COUNTRY / MARKET:
Italy (default). If the best-known pathway is abroad, say the country in the narration and give the Italian equivalent when it exists.

TARGET DURATION:
80–100 seconds.

TARGET WORD COUNT:
Spoken Italian ≈ 150 words per minute → **200–250 words**. Hard limit: never above 260. A short must be cut, not rushed.

---

## STEP 1 — PRIVATE PROFILE, THEN CUT HARD

Identify entry route, real Italian titles / livelli CCNL / gradi, licences, tools, risks, pay model and costs. Then keep **only one striking detail per level**. A short cannot hold a profile; it holds a ladder with the best number, the best word and the best cost at each step.

---

## STEP 2 — SHORT STORY ARCHITECTURE

Choose **4–5 levels** (never more). Each level is a real jump in competence, authority, pay, risk or personal cost. Skip the in-between steps and say so in one phrase if needed ("saltiamo qualche anno").

Fixed skeleton, with approximate time budget:

1. **Hook (0–5 s, ≤ 20 words):** one number or one contrast that creates a question. Start with the pay gap, a risk, or a sharp fact, with no greeting and no "in questo video". Example pattern: "A diciannove anni guadagni X. A quarantacinque, Y. Ecco cosa cambia in mezzo."
2. **Level 1 → Level 4/5 (≈ 12–15 s each, 30–40 words):** for each level give, in this order of priority:
   * the **title or grado** (real name);
   * the **pay**, stating its type (lordo / netto / fatturato) and how it is earned;
   * **one concrete task, tool or danger**;
   * **one cost or uncomfortable truth** (health, time, family, debt, risk).
   Vary which element leads at each level; do not run the same four beats in the same order four times.
3. **Hidden cost (≈ 8 s):** the one price of the career nobody mentions.
4. **Closing (≈ 5–8 s):** return to the opening image or number, and land one contrast that belongs only to this profession.
5. **Subscription invitation:** one short line, natural, integrated in the closing, no longer than 12 words (es. "Iscriviti se ti sei mai chiesto cosa serve davvero per arrivare in cima.").

Second person ("tu"). The viewer climbs the ladder; the voice never describes from the outside.

---

## STEP 3 — WRITING RULES FOR A SHORT

* **One idea per line. Each line 4–10 words, one breath**, so it can be a subtitle and a visual change every 2–3 seconds.
* The important number or word goes **at the end of the line** (the emphasis lands on the last words).
* A new hook, reversal or number **every 10–15 seconds**; no sentence that only explains.
* Real insider vocabulary: at most 2–3 terms in the whole script, each made understandable by the very sentence it appears in.
* Numbers written for speaking ("ventotto mila euro lordi l'anno").
* No generic motivation, no clichés ("sudore e sacrificio"), no stacked subordinates, no filler transitions.
* Never mention steps you cannot afford. If a level needs more than 40 words to be fair, remove another level.

---

## STEP 4 — FACTUAL INTEGRITY (STRICT)

* Use realistic **ranges** and always state the figure type: lordo, netto, tariffa oraria, fatturato, utile.
* Keep employee pay, overtime and allowances, contract income, commission, revenue and personal profit separate.
* Verify current or country-specific figures with reliable sources first: CCNL, ISTAT, INPS, INAIL, Ministero del Lavoro, ENAC, Ministero Difesa / Interno, bandi di concorso, Ordini professionali, sindacati, enti di formazione, reputable industry publications.
* Never invent experts, quotes, regulations, statistics or anecdotes. If a number cannot be verified, qualify it with a range or leave it out.
* Original wording only; nothing copied from another channel or source.
* No instruction that would help bypass safety rules, licences or regulations.
* In a short, a wrong number is the biggest risk: prefer fewer numbers, all verified.

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Write **only the spoken narration** in Italian: one idea per line, no blank lines, no headings, bullets, timestamps, stage directions or markdown.

After the final line, provide exactly:

TOTAL WORD COUNT: [NUMBER]
ESTIMATED DURATION: [SECONDS, at about 150 words per minute]
LEVELS COVERED: [LIST IN ORDER]
MAIN SOURCES: [LIST, with the type of figure each one supports]

Then write exactly:

## SELF-CHECK BEFORE DELIVERING (PRIVATE, DO NOT PRINT)

* 200–250 words (never above 260); estimated 80–100 s.
* The hook has a number or a contrast within 20 words, with no greeting.
* 4–5 levels only, each with title, pay (and type), one task or danger, one cost.
* One hidden cost, and an ending that returns to the opening and lands a contrast specific to this profession.
* Subscription line present, ≤ 12 words, inside the closing.
* Every line is 4–10 words and can be said in one breath; the key number or word ends the line.
* A new hook or reversal at least every 15 seconds.
* All numbers verified or qualified, with the figure type stated; no invented source.
* Numbers written for speaking; natural spoken Italian.
